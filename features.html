✦ INDIA COMPLIANCE

Built for the new Indian rulebook

The labour codes in force from 21 November 2025, the Income-tax Act 2025 from 1 April 2026 and the 2026 PF wage-ceiling change are in the engine as dated rules, so each month is worked out on the rule in force that month.

Labour-code wages

The Code on Wages definition of wages with the 50% test, applied when your pay policy turns it on, shown step by step so you can see why a figure came out as it did.

PF, EPS and VPF

EPF and EPS split, the ₹25,000 wage ceiling from 17 September 2026 (and the earlier ₹15,000 for older months), voluntary PF, and international workers with or without a Certificate of Coverage. The ECR text file is checked for gaps before you upload.

ESI, professional tax and LWF

ESI at the wage threshold with statutory round-up, state-wise professional tax slabs including the February and gender rules, and labour welfare fund by state.

Income tax and Form 130

Section 192 projection under the old and new regimes for FY 2026-27, declarations with proofs, HRA, 80C and employer NPS, and the year’s tax certificate (Form 130, formerly Form 16) as a PDF for each employee, or all of them in one zip with an e-mail telling people it is ready. Foreign tax credit under a treaty and interest on taxed employer contributions are supported, and flagged as awaiting adviser sign-off.

TDS returns and challans

The quarterly return file (Form 138, formerly 24Q) for validation in the FVU utility, with challan records, month-by-month reconciliation of tax deducted against tax deposited, late-deposit interest and the token number once filed.

Perquisites, ESOP and Form 123

Employer-provided benefits valued and taxed through payroll, ESOP and RSU exercises, deferred start-up option tax and employer-paid tax tracked with its challan in the TDS register, and the perquisite statement (Form 123, formerly 12BA) per employee.

Minimum wages and registers

A state-wise minimum-wage check on every run, the register of wages (Form IV), and PF, ESI, PT, LWF and TDS summaries for your records.

Settlements and bonus

Final settlement with leave encashment, notice pay, gratuity and recoveries; statutory bonus; arrears recalculated for earlier months with PF, ESI and PT on the difference.

When the law changes

A new rate or slab is a reviewed rule with an effective date and a source note. Each rule shows who has verified it.

Nominations and PF declaration

PF, pension, ESI and gratuity nominations (Forms 2, 1 and F) and the PF declaration (Form 11) captured from the employee, checked (shares add to 100%, guardians for minors), and shown in the registers.

New-joiner registration sheet

A ready list of people who still need a UAN or an ESI IP number, with the details the EPFO and ESIC portals ask for and what is missing on each record.

Unpaid leave after a month is final

Approve or cancel unpaid leave in a finalized month: the run stays as it was and the difference comes back as arrears in the next run, with PF, ESI and PT on it.

Statutory figures and file layouts are versioned rules with a source note. Where one has not yet been confirmed by a tax or PF adviser it is marked as such in the product, and we recommend your adviser reviews the rules before go-live. Form 130 Part A (from TRACES) is downloaded from TRACES, not produced here.

✦ ATTENDANCE, SHIFTS AND OVERTIME

Pay that follows the days people worked

Record daily attendance from punches, a sheet or your time system, and let absent days and approved overtime flow into the run with no re-typing.

Daily attendance

Present, absent, half day, late, leave, weekly off and holiday for every person, worked out from in and out punches against their shift and the pay group’s working week.

Shifts and rosters

Day and night shifts with break, grace period and full or half-day rules, usual shift per person from a date, and a different shift for a single day.

Three ways in

Mark days by hand, import a CSV or Excel sheet (checked first, row by row), or push punches from a biometric or time system through the API with a dry run.

Overtime, your way

Minimum minutes, rounding, daily cap, approval, a rate for working days, weekly offs and holidays, and a quarterly-limit warning. HR can change the rates for a month while processing it.

Release to payroll

Every working day needs a status before the month is released. Loss-of-pay days and approved overtime then reach the run, and a released month can be reopened until the run is finalized.

Leave in the same place

Leave types, balances, accruals, year-end encashment and unpaid leave that becomes loss of pay, with approvals by the manager.

✦ FINANCE INTEGRATION

Straight into your books and your bank

Every finalized run becomes a balanced journal your finance team can post, and the bank file is ready when approval is done.

Monthly journal by cost centre

One balanced voucher per month: salaries and employer costs debited by cost centre, net pay, PF, ESI, PT, TDS and recoveries credited. A month that does not balance is not exported.

Tally, Zoho Books, CSV and JSON

Tally masters and journal voucher files, a Zoho Books journal CSV and API request, and generic CSV or JSON for QuickBooks, Xero, SAP or Dynamics. Each hand-over is recorded.

Finance API

Your finance system can fetch the journal itself with a key that has the journal scope. Ledger names map to your chart of accounts, and a component can post to its own ledger.

Bank files

Disbursement files for HDFC, ICICI, a generic CSV or a layout you define, validated for IFSC, account length, duplicates and control totals, and sealed with a hash.

Separate books per entity

With several legal entities, the journal and statutory returns can be produced for one entity at a time.

Export layouts for Tally and Zoho Books follow their published formats and have not been tested against every customer setup; we recommend a trial import on a test company first.

✦ LETTERS AND DOCUMENTS

Letters with a reference and a way to verify them

Issue the letters HR writes most, keep a register, and let employees see theirs.

Offer, relieving, experience, salary certificate

Generated from your live data: the offer’s salary break-up comes from your pay policy, the salary certificate from the latest finalized run, and the experience letter from the employment history.

Your wording

Edit the text of each letter with checked placeholders, or return to the standard wording.

Reference and verification

Every letter has a number and a verification code. Anyone holding it can confirm the letter is genuine on a public page, or see that it was withdrawn.

Hold, release, withdraw

Keep a letter back until it is checked, release it to the employee with an email, or withdraw it with a reason. All of it stays in the register.

Employee documents

Letters appear in the employee’s portal under My letters and Documents, alongside proofs and papers they upload themselves.

✦ EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE

Everything an employee needs, on their phone

Employees sign in with their work email link or Microsoft account and see only their own data.

Payslips and tax

Payslips as PDFs, tax declaration with proof uploads, a live view of their tax working and their year-end certificate.

My attendance

Their month at a glance, punch in and out when the company allows it, and requests to correct a day.

Leave

Balances, applying for leave and withdrawing a request.

Claims and benefits

Expense claims with bills, reimbursements and flexible benefit elections within their entitlement.

My letters

Salary certificates, experience letters and others released by HR, with a verification code.

Profile and bank changes

Details and bank-account changes go through approval before payroll uses them, with a photo and documents.

Assistant and help

An assistant that searches help written for employees and answers about their own leave and requests.

Declare every deduction

Guided forms for rent (HRA), leave travel, home loan, 80C, 80D, 80G, 80E, NPS, disability and more, with the few details HR needs to check the proof, and checks that catch mistakes before they reach HR.

Form 130 in the portal

Download the year’s tax certificate once the financial year ends and payroll has run for it, with every download recorded.

Nominations

Name PF, pension, ESI and gratuity nominees and fill in the PF declaration in the portal, with a list of what is still missing.

✦ MANAGER SELF-SERVICE

Managers decide without leaving the portal

Anyone with direct reports gets a manager view in the same portal.

Team view

Direct reports with role, department and status, and a team leave calendar.

Approvals in one place

Leave requests and attendance corrections of their team, approved or rejected with a note.

Change requests

Raise a pay or role change or an exit for a team member, which HR then approves; employees raise their own resignation.

Claims of the team

Approve or reject expense claims of direct reports, in full or for less, with the bill alongside and the category limits still applied.

Team overtime

Approve overtime days for a month, in full, for fewer minutes or not at all, until HR releases the month to payroll.

✦ ADMIN PANEL

The whole company, configured by your team

No tickets to us for routine changes.

Company setup

Locations, departments, designations, grades, holidays, employer bank accounts, tax details, registrations and legal entities.

Pay groups and structures

Monthly, fortnightly and weekly groups, salary structures and components, compensation plans and revisions, and pay-policy versions with approval.

Smart import

Bring employees in from CSV or Excel with column matching and a check before anything is saved, or from Microsoft Entra.

Run workbench

Inputs, exceptions inbox, review, sign-off, approval and finalization, bank file and payslip release, with a timeline of who did what.

Reports centre

Payroll register, cost by department, liability, loss of pay, variable pay, TDS statements, PT annual, PF, ESI, LWF and the wage register, with exports.

What-if scenarios

Try a salary revision or a policy change on real data before you commit to it.

Run inputs from a sheet

Upload a CSV or Excel sheet of loss-of-pay days, TDS, notes and each variable component. It is checked first, row by row, and nothing is saved unless every row is right.

Registers with nominees

The register of employees carries father’s or husband’s name and nominees, with a nominations register and a count of people who have not named one.

✦ THE ASSISTANT

An assistant that knows the whole product

Ask in plain words. It answers from your own data and the product’s help, shows what it will do on a card, and changes nothing until you press Confirm. It only sees what your role may see.

Why is it zero?

“Why was no TDS deducted for Asha?” It finds the rule and the condition behind a figure, and works out one person’s tax under both regimes.

Run payroll with you

Check what blocks a run, compare versions, prepare, calculate and submit one step at a time. A second person still approves.

Checks before you file

PF, ESI and TDS files are checked for what is missing or odd before they go to the portals.

Reports by asking

“Show the October register”, “who has not named a PF nominee?”: it opens the right report with the right filters.

Set-up by conversation

“Change casual leave to 10 days”, “add a holiday”: a card to confirm, an undo, and pay-policy changes go through back-test and approval.

Advice on every page

A page review says what to fix, what to consider and what is fine for your pay policy, leave rules, people data, runs, claims and tax set-up.

Rules, never from memory

Current slabs, PF and ESI ceilings, professional tax and minimum wages are read from the rules page, with what is still unverified said plainly.

For employees and managers

Apply for leave, check balances, raise a claim, declare 80C, see request status, raise a ticket; managers see who is away and act on what waits.

Tells you what it can do

Ask “what can you help with?” and it lists what it can look up, explain, prepare and check for your role, and what it cannot do yet.

The assistant never sees another company’s data, never types identifier numbers into the model and never approves or files on its own.

✦ ROLES AND SECURITY

Least access, recent sign-in and a full trail

Controls an auditor can ask about.

Roles that fit your team

Owner, HR manager, HR admin and payroll admin, plus custom roles built from a permission list. A person can hold several roles and cannot grant more than they have.

Recent sign-in for sensitive steps

Approving or finalizing a run, generating a bank file, changing roles or bank accounts and issuing API keys need a recent sign-in, with a window you set.

Maker and checker

Decide per area whether a different person must approve pay runs, pay policy, recoveries and bank changes. When an owner approves their own work it is recorded as self-approved.

Audit trail

Who changed what and when, with old and new values for roles and security, and every export, bank file and use of AI marked by its source.

Data protection

PAN, bank account and UAN numbers are stored encrypted and shown masked; each company’s data is isolated at the database level; searches never match on identifiers or salary. Hosted in India.

API keys with scopes

Per-company keys limited to what an integration needs, and rate limited.

✦ GROUPS AND SWITCHING

Several companies, mid-year starts, all handled

The cases that usually need a consultant.

Several legal entities

Each employing company keeps its own PAN, TAN, GSTIN, address, EPF, ESI and PT registrations and tax returns, and the pay group chooses who employs whom. Payslips and certificates carry the right employer.

Switching mid-year

Import each person’s earnings and tax from the old provider for the months already paid, so tax for the rest of the year and Form 130 carry on correctly. Checked first, and reversible.

Open API

Employees, attendance (monthly or daily punches), leave sync and the finance journal, with idempotency keys, batches and a dry run.
