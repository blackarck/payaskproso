✦ INDIA STATUTORY ENGINE

Indian Compliance, Built Into the Engine

PF, ESI, Professional Tax and TDS are versioned rules with effective dates — not spreadsheets, and not tribal knowledge.

PF, ESI & Professional Tax

EPF/EPS split with the ₹15,000 wage ceiling (or actual-wage mode), ESI at the ₹21,000 threshold with statutory round-up, and state-wise PT slabs across 28 states, including gender and February rules such as Maharashtra's.

TDS, Form 16 & 24Q

Section 192 projection across the old and new regimes (115BAC), investment declarations and proofs, quarterly 24Q e-TDS returns and digitally signed Form 16 Part A & B.

When the Law Changes, We Update Rules

A new rate or slab is published as a reviewed, back-tested rule with an effective date. No software release, no surprises on pay day. AI drafts the change; a human approves it.
